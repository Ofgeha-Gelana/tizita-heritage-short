## Notes on measurement

- `latency_ms` is the wall time of the HTTP call, or of the job inside the Kaggle kernel. It does not include Kaggle queue time, which is logged separately as the poll duration in the run output.
- `gpu_s` is taken as the job's wall time on the GPU. Model loading (once per kernel) appears in the kernel log, not per job.
- A T4 is priced at about $0.35 per GPU-hour, a typical on-demand cloud rate, to give a list-price equivalent for the GPU stages.
